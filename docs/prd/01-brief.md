# Brief

## What it is

TechTank TO (TechTank) is a volunteer-run, Toronto-based Canadian tech community founded in
2023. The redesign moves the site away from a flat "link-tree" layout toward a
conversion-oriented onboarding hub that turns curious visitors into active contributors —
attendees, speakers, hosts, sponsors, and organizers.

The site stays content-driven marketing: it doesn't own accounts or ticketing. It funnels
visitors to external platforms (Luma, Meetup, Slack, LinkedIn, GitHub, YouTube, Instagram)
and captures intake via email to `techtankto@gmail.com`.

## Mission and pillars

Foster a supportive and inclusive environment where people of all skill levels can explore,
create, and thrive in technology, built on four pillars: **Community** (belonging, safety,
inclusivity), **Innovation** (curiosity, learning in public, experimentation), **Teamwork**
(collaboration over competition), **Respect** (enforced via the Code of Conduct).

## Who it's for

- Software developers at all career levels (junior to senior).
- Designers, PMs, tech-adjacent roles.
- Experienced professionals open to mentoring or speaking.
- Companies wanting to host, sponsor, or recruit.
- Media, partners, and sponsors looking for brand assets.

## Event types

Tech talks, build nights (currently Build Night TO by TechTank x PostHog), socials, sports,
Code Diversity (inclusive events for underrepresented groups in tech), panels, and workshops
(planned). Most events are free; some (workshops, special activities) may have associated
costs — never advertise all events as free.

## Product goals

1. Build social trust via social proof — testimonials, event photography, sponsor/host logo
   cloud.
2. Convert visitors into specific roles through a dedicated onboarding funnel
   (`/get-involved/*`).
3. Keep event discovery effortless (embedded Luma calendar).
4. Provide a professional, one-stop resource for press and sponsors.
5. Centralize legal/compliance docs cleanly.

## Canadian context

A Canadian tech community based in Toronto, Ontario, serving the Greater Toronto Area tech
ecosystem under Ontario/Canadian jurisdiction. External links to sponsor/partner companies
should use Canadian variants where available (`.ca` domains, `/en-ca` paths). Current
sponsors and hosts: 7shifts, Rakuten Canada, Cohere, Docebo, Vena Solutions, Points /
Plusgrade, Intuit Canada, Microsoft Canada, BrainStation, Prema Coffee.

## Success metrics

Intake emails per role (speaker/host/sponsor/organizer); RSVP click-throughs to Luma; Press
Kit downloads; scroll depth and CTA click-through rate on `/` and `/about`; returning
visitors and newsletter/Slack joins.

## Aesthetic

Professional and welcoming — not corporate, not casual. Credible enough that a sponsor's
marketing team feels comfortable associating their brand, while staying open and approachable
to a first-time attendee from any background. Confident whitespace, a clean type scale;
nothing playful or youthful. Keep the aquatic motif (fish logo, "Tank" vocabulary) as a
subtle mark, not the dominant motif. Real event photography over stock illustration.
